# Dogfood: runtime-chosen settings, round 4

Rules: `round4.md` (round 2 where it does not say otherwise). Changed programs are in `programs-r4/`. The rest are in `programs-r2/`. Axes: O obvious, D discoverable, C concise, S safe by default, P complete. A score under 5 names the line.

Python, Go, Rust, TypeScript, and Swift pass a zone or a pool as an argument. Dewy keeps one setting for the scope. That is why `today` and `run()` stay at 4. A looser function cap running at the tighter one is a choice this round keeps, so `wider` and `index` stay at 4 too. It is not dynamic scope forcing it.

## Floor

4. The lowest lines are the ones the rules leave in the source: `run()` does not repeat the cap (`01:5`), `today` does not take a zone (`05:5`), `wider` says 16 and runs at 8 (`09:4`), and `$runtime_checks = debug` is an error (`R2:10`). Raising those means a new argument, a new member, or making the looser cap a compile error. None of those is this round. `available_memory` is the member that would lift `03:5`. It is proposed in `round4.md` and not adopted: no cold read.
